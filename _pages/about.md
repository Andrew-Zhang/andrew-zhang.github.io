I am a PhD student in Computer Science at UCLA, advised by [Aditya Grover](https://aditya-grover.github.io/). Previously, I worked on accelerating inference on [AWS Trainium](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/machine-learning/trainium/) devices. I did a BS/MS at Virginia Tech under [Chris Thomas](https://people.cs.vt.edu/chris/). I am interested in multi-token generation (discrete diffusion and speculative decoding) and hardware-aware algorithms.
